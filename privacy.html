Privacy Policy

LuvChat: AI Character Chat (package com.luvchat.aichat.lovechat.character) · Effective date: November 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AHV Modnix ("we", "us") collects, uses and shares information when you use the mobile application LuvChat: AI Character Chat ("the App"). By installing or using the App you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Who may use the App

The App is intended for adults. You must be at least 18 years old to use it. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide. The App does not require an account. During setup you may enter a display name, age range, preferred chat style and an avatar (chosen from built-in images or your own photo via the system photo picker). Messages you type in chat are processed to generate the AI character's reply.

Information collected automatically. When you use the App we and our service providers may collect: device model and operating system version, app version, language, country/region, IP address, crash and diagnostic logs, usage events (screens opened, features used, ads viewed), and the Android Advertising ID (resettable identifier).

Photos. If you choose an avatar from your gallery, the App uses the Android photo picker; only the single image you select is accessed and it is stored on your device.

3. How we use information

  • To provide and operate the App, including generating AI character replies to your messages.
  • To remember your preferences (name, age range, style, avatar, language, matched characters, chat history) on your device.
  • To show advertising and measure its performance.
  • To analyze usage, fix crashes and improve the App.
  • To measure the effectiveness of our marketing campaigns (install attribution).
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms.

4. AI-generated content

Replies in the App are generated by an automated AI model. Your chat messages are sent to our AI service provider to produce a reply. Messages are processed to generate responses and to maintain the context of the conversation; they are not used to identify you personally. AI characters are fictional. Do not share sensitive personal information (financial details, government IDs, health information) in chats.

5. Third-party services

The App integrates the following third-party SDKs, each of which may collect information under its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob and mediation partners (such as Google Ad Manager, ironSource, Mintegral, Pangle, Chartboost) — advertising. Google Privacy Policy, AdMob partners.
  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Remote Config, Crash reporting) — analytics, configuration, stability. Firebase Privacy.
  • Meta (Facebook) SDK — app events for marketing measurement. Meta Privacy Policy.
  • Adjust — install attribution and ad revenue measurement. Adjust Privacy Policy.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) — consent management in regions where required.

These providers may use the Advertising ID, IP address and device information to serve personalized or non-personalized ads and to measure attribution. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID and opt out of ads personalization in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

6. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with: (a) the service providers listed above, for the purposes described; (b) our AI service provider, to generate chat replies; (c) authorities when required by law; and (d) a successor in the event of a merger or acquisition, under this Policy.

7. Data retention

Profile settings, matched characters and chat history are stored on your device and are removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data. Analytics and advertising data are retained by the respective providers according to their policies (Firebase Analytics: up to 14 months by default). Diagnostic logs are retained for up to 90 days.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Delete local data at any time by clearing the App's data or uninstalling it.
  • Reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in Android settings.
  • Where you are in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, you can change your ad consent through the consent form shown in the App.
  • Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of your personal data, or to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. To exercise them, contact us using the details below.

9. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS). We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

10. International transfers

Our service providers may process information on servers located outside your country, including the United States. By using the App you consent to such transfers, which are protected by the providers' safeguards.

We may update this Policy from time to time. The effective date above shows the latest version. Material changes will be announced in the App or on this page.
